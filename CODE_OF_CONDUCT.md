# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Welcome people with different levels of experience, critique ideas rather than people, and protect private engineering data shared during debugging.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's confidential material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the GitHub profile for `SouravBeraAkaSpeed`.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
